1. The Challenge

Africa's Diagnostic Bottleneck

The Infrastructure Gap

Over 80 percent of community pharmacies and primary care clinics cannot fund multi-million-naira laboratory analyzers, limiting point-of-care diagnostics for millions.

The Liquidity Gap

Diagnostics often require full upfront payment. Patients skip tests and self-medicate based on symptoms, fueling misdiagnosis, treatment failure, and long-term costs.

Distribution Challenges

Manufacturers and reagent suppliers struggle to reach smaller providers due to fragmented channels, logistics inefficiencies, and high last-mile costs.

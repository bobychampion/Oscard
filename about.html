About Us

People you can count on.

Oscar Diagnostics is a subsidiary of SOSOCARE — Nigeria's leading micro health insurance platform. We exist to make accurate, affordable diagnostics accessible to every Nigerian.

Who We Are

A laboratory built on precision and compassion

Oscar Diagnostics is a state-of-the-art medical laboratory serving individuals, families, and healthcare providers. As a proud subsidiary of SOSOCARE, we share the same mission: to close the gap in healthcare access across Africa.

We combine NABL-accredited standards with a compassionate, patient-first approach — so every test we run is not just accurate, but meaningful to the life behind the sample.

Research & Innovation

Advancing human health through technology

Beyond diagnostics, Oscar Diagnostics is actively involved in medical research aimed at improving human health and development. We sit at the intersection of laboratory science and med-tech — exploring how technology can make diagnosis faster, cheaper, and more predictive.

Our research focuses on population health data, early disease detection models, and scalable diagnostic infrastructure for underserved communities across Nigeria and Africa.
